### Added
- A Tasks section at the top of the sidebar and the phone's drawer, on the herdr-core runtime, when
  this PC serves Shepherdr: Shepherdr's own groups (Needs you, Ready, Working, Done), order, counts
  and words from its glance feed, read on this PC with its local token. A row opens its task's
  terminal in chat view, or says the task runs on another machine or has no terminal running; the
  drawer button shows Shepherdr's Needs you count. While a task is listed, its terminals (its agent,
  helpers and reviewers) leave the workspace list, the agent list and the Inbox. A short drop of the
  feed keeps the last list for about two minutes, marked reconnecting.
